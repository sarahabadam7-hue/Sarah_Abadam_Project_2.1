Portfolio: proposal and planning

Intent

This portfolio exists to showcase my work as an artist and designer through a deliberately minimalist approach. Every decision, from layout and typography to navigation and image presentation, is made to keep attention on the work itself. Together, these qualities present me as a capable, creative, and thoughtful individual who understands that good design is often a matter of restraint. The site is intended to serve as both a record of my practice and a clear introduction to what I can offer.

Audience

The primary audience is employers, as well as established artists and designers who may wish to evaluate my skills, collaborate, or offer feedback. For this group, the site provides a clear and efficient view of my abilities, with work organised so that its range and quality are easy to assess.

The site also speaks to aspiring artists and designers. By presenting a diverse selection of work across different areas of the design industry, it aims to show how broad and varied creative practice can be. It also seeks to encourage artistic expression as a worthwhile hobby, not only as a career, and to inspire visitors to explore their own creativity.

Tone

The tone of the site is professional and formal. All written content is grammatically correct, carefully edited, and considerate of the reader, and it avoids language that could be careless, dismissive, or exclusionary. Communication is clear, succinct, and purposeful, so that visitors always know what they are viewing and where to go next.

Above all, the tone projects confidence and competence. I aim to speak about my work with assurance and honesty, acknowledging its strengths without boasting and remaining open to growth and feedback. The result should feel self-assured but never arrogant, and accomplished but never complacent.
